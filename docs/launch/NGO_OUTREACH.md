# NGO & Community Partnership Package

For GBV NGOs, youth organisations, community groups, clinics, faith
communities, universities and workplaces.

**The position is not "please advertise our app".** The position is:

> Fast is a free digital resource organisations can give to the people they
> serve — for prevention information, safety planning and verified support
> resources — at zero cost, with zero data collection, on any phone.

Fast never claims an organisation as a partner, supporter or endorser until
that organisation confirms in writing. Nothing in this package asks for
money, branding, or endorsements.

## 2. Formal partnership proposal

### What Fast is

A free, independent, public-interest web platform (Progressive Web App)
that consolidates verified GBV support resources and prevention education
for South Africa. Every emergency number is maintained in a single verified
source in the codebase, protected by automated integrity tests. Statistics
and news are shown only with their publishing organisation and a real
source URL — ungrounded claims are dropped by the system, not just by
policy.

### The problem it solves for organisations

Your staff meet people who need information at moments your organisation
isn't open, isn't reachable, or isn't the right first door. Fast is the
resource that stays available in those gaps: 24/7, offline-capable,
anonymous. It complements your services; it never replaces them.

### What Fast offers the people you serve

- **Emergency access:** GBV Command Centre, SAPS, emergency services,
  please-call-me and SMS silent channels — all in one tap, even offline.
- **Prevention education:** warning signs, coercive control, consent,
  boundaries, digital abuse, healthy relationships.
- **Safety planning:** private, step-by-step, stored only on the person's
  device.
- **Pathways to your world:** shelters, counselling, medical and legal
  support presented with service names and hours as the services publish
  them.
- **Behaviour change:** a programme for people who want to stop harming —
  an underserved doorway that most services cannot afford to hold open.

### What Fast offers your organisation

- A free, zero-maintenance digital resource you can distribute by poster,
  card or message.
- Content you can hand to a board or committee without data-protection
  risk: Fast stores no personal information on any server.
- Materials ready to use (this folder): one-pager, FAQ, posters, messages.

### What we ask

Use it if it helps. Tell us what's missing. Point out anything that should
be corrected. There is no fee, no MOU requirement, no branding obligation,
and no request for beneficiary data — ever.

### Honest limits

Fast is an independent project, not a government service. Its live figures
come from a grounded, source-linked pipeline and fall back to a verified
baseline. It is English today, with the localisation architecture in place
for reviewed translations. It is not a replacement for professional
counselling, medical care, policing or legal advice.

### Distribution methods that work (see section 5)

QR posters in safe-visibility places; the WhatsApp message set; a line in
your newsletters; cards at reception.

## 4. Partnership FAQ

**Is Fast affiliated with government or any NGO network?**
No. It is an independent public-interest project that links people to the
official services. It displays no endorsements and accepts none until
confirmed in writing.

**Does it collect data on the people we serve?**
No. There are no accounts, no tracking cookies and no server-side profiles.
The optional personalisation (a greeting) lives in the visitor's own
browser and is removed with two taps. Fast cannot sell, leak or subpoena
what it never collected.

**Who verifies the emergency numbers?**
The numbers live in one verified source file with an automated integrity
test; the human verification workflow (what to check, where, how often,
who approves) is documented in the repository's
EMERGENCY_DIRECTORY_VERIFICATION process. If a service changes its number,
the site changes in one place.

**Can we brand it for our organisation?**
The site itself stays unbranded and neutral — that neutrality is what makes
it shareable by everyone. But your poster can sit next to your logo: the QR
links to Fast; the trust comes from you.

**What if someone in crisis comes to us through Fast?**
Fast never routes people to organisations — it routes to the services'
published contact channels. People who reach you through a Fast poster are
your visitors, and Fast keeps no record of them.

**Does it cost anything, ever?**
No fees, no premium tier, no advertising. The site runs on hosting that
costs less per month than a print run, by design.

**Can we translate it for our community?**
The localisation architecture exists and translations ship only after human
review of crisis terminology. **HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED:** if your organisation
has reviewed translators, contact the maintainers — machine translation of
safety content is deliberately not shipped.

**What does "works offline" mean exactly?**
Once a person has opened Fast, the page and the emergency directory remain
available without data. The live statistics refresh when a connection
exists; the crisis path never depends on one.

## 5. Suggested ways an organisation can distribute Fast

1. **Reception QR card** — at sign-in desks and waiting areas.
2. **Bathroom stall posters** — the one place people look privately.
3. **Noticeboard poster** — community halls, youth centres, staff rooms.
4. **WhatsApp broadcast to your own consented lists** — use messages 1–10
   from [WHATSAPP_CAMPAIGN.md](WHATSAPP_CAMPAIGN.md).
5. **Workshop closer** — end every awareness session with "save this now,
   on a good day" and ten quiet seconds to do it.
6. **Newsletter line** — "A free, private resource your family can save:
   [FAST URL]".
7. **Counsellor cards** — hand-sized cards with the QR for private
   handover, no conversation required.
8. **Faith-community notice sheets** — one line + QR after services.
9. **University residences** — corridor boards and orientation packs.
10. **Clinic waiting rooms** — poster #2, next to general health posters.
