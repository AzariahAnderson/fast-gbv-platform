# FAST — 16 Days / 16 Actions

One calm, practical action per day from **Wednesday 25 November** to
**Thursday 10 December 2026** (Human Rights Day), the 16 Days of Activism
Against Gender-Based Violence and Femicide window. Each day educates first
and points to Fast second. Nothing here is fear-based; nothing claims a
statistic that is not already sourced and linked on the Fast live section.

**Voice:** calm · human · trustworthy · empowering · South African · practical.
**Never:** "YOU COULD BE NEXT", "BEFORE IT'S TOO LATE", "GBV IS EVERYWHERE".

**Standing CTA destinations on the site:** `#emergency` (verified directory),
`#survivors` (support pathways), `#live` (sourced figures and news),
`#change` (behaviour-change programme), `#faq`, plus the pinned
"Get help now" crisis call button.

**Visual system:** the four Fast colours only — deep ink `#454040`,
taupe `#605B51`, gold `#D8D365`, lime `#E6F082`; Inter typography; generous
whitespace; one 3D animated emoji maximum per visual. No stock photos of
distressed women, no black-and-blue bruises, no broken-glass metaphors.

## Campaign operations notes

- Post timing: mornings 07:00–09:00 or evenings 18:00–20:00 SAST, when
  mobile data bundles are used and feeds are calm.
- Every post answers comments with the same three rules: believe, inform,
  point to the verified directory — never diagnose, never promise outcomes.
- If a post attracts someone in active danger, respond privately with the
  GBV Command Centre number exactly as displayed on the site and stop
  engaging publicly. The [AMBASSADOR_PROGRAM.md](AMBASSADOR_PROGRAM.md)
  urgent-help protocol applies to every admin account.
- Do not boost fear-based content others tag Fast into; repost the calm
  originals instead.
- After 10 December: rotate to the evergreen weekly rhythm in
  [SOCIAL_CONTENT.md](SOCIAL_CONTENT.md) — the site remains useful every
  day of the year.
