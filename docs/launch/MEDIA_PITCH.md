# Local Media Package

Everything a community newspaper, radio station or journalist needs — short,
checkable and honest. Fast makes no claims of national impact, user counts,
government endorsement or partnerships anywhere in this package.

# South African developer launches FAST, a free digital platform focused on GBV prevention and safety

**[City, Province]** — A South African developer has launched **FAST**
(Family Assistance & Safety Technology), a free web platform that gathers
the country's gender-based violence support services, prevention education
and safety-planning guidance into a single, calm, privacy-first page.

FAST puts the verified GBV Command Centre, police and emergency numbers,
and silent please-call-me and SMS channels one tap away on any phone. It
works without an account, collects no personal information on any server,
and keeps working offline once a person has opened it — a deliberate choice
for a country where airtime, signal and privacy cannot be assumed.

The platform includes plain-language prevention education — warning signs,
coercive control, consent, boundaries, digital abuse and healthy
relationships — a private, step-by-step safety-planning guide, and a
programme aimed at people who want to stop causing harm. Statistics and
news shown on the platform are regenerated hourly from grounded searches
and display their publishing organisation and a real source link; anything
that cannot be verified is dropped rather than displayed.

"FAST exists because finding the right number in the worst moment is hard,"

> FAST began as a practical question: when someone in South Africa decides,
> in the worst five minutes of their life, to look for help — what do they
> actually find? Searching scatters answers across dozens of pages; some
> are outdated, some unsafe to open at work or at home, some ask for things
> a person in danger cannot give: an account, a download, a history.

## 4. Interview talking points (10)

1. **The gap:** finding the right number in the worst moment is the hardest
   step; FAST removes the search, not the services.
2. **One page, one tap:** Command Centre, police, emergency services and
   silent channels — verified and dialable, no scrolling through PDFs.
3. **Silent channels matter:** please-call-me and SMS exist for people who
   cannot make a voice call safely; FAST makes them visible and easy.
4. **Privacy is a safety feature:** no accounts, no server-side personal
   data, no tracking — safe to open in a monitored household; the optional
   greeting lives only on the device and is removed in two taps.
5. **Offline by design:** once opened, the crisis path keeps working with
   no signal and no airtime — because crises do not check signal bars.
6. **Honest numbers:** statistics and news regenerate hourly and always
   carry their publishing organisation and a real source link; unverifiable
   claims are dropped automatically rather than shown.
7. **Prevention, not just crisis:** plain-language education on warning
   signs, coercive control, consent, boundaries and digital abuse.
8. **Change is possible:** FAST includes a programme for people who want to
   stop harming — a doorway most services cannot keep open.
9. **Built as a public good:** independent, non-government, no advertising,
   no data to sell; the business model is that there isn't one.
10. **The ask is small:** save it on a good day, share it with one person.
    Stay close. Stay safe.
