# Social Content Library

Sixty concepts, education-first. Fast appears as the calm answer at the
end — never the punchline at the start. Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels,
Instagram posts, Facebook, LinkedIn.

**Global rules**
- Palette only: ink `#454040`, taupe `#605B51`, gold `#D8D365`, lime `#E6F082`; Inter; one 3D emoji max.
- No distressed faces, no bruises, no blood, no broken-glass, no "she should have left" framing.
- Every caption ends with one CTA and the link `[FAST URL]` (replace when live — **HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED**).
- Emergency numbers are never typed in captions; the site is the source.
- Formats: **V** = short-form video (15–40s) · **P** = static post · **C** = carousel · **Q** = quote/statement graphic.
