# WhatsApp Distribution System

WhatsApp is South Africa's town square: the fastest honest way to move Fast
from one person to another. These messages are written to be **forwarded**,
not broadcast. Send them from a personal number, one at a time, to people
who will actually want them — never to groups that did not ask, never in
bulk, never with more than one exclamation mark.

**Rules for every message**
- Short enough to read on one screen (under ~450 characters).
- One idea, one link. The link is always the real production URL — `[FAST URL]`
  until the domain is configured. **HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED:** replace before sending.
- Numbers only as displayed on the site. Never retype an emergency number
  from memory into a message; the site stays the single source of truth.
- No pressure, no fear, no "forward this to 10 people".

## Forwarding mechanics

- Ask people to forward, not screenshot: the link stays tappable.
- Never attach the emergency numbers as an image; images cannot be dialled
  and the numbers can drift from the verified directory. The site is the source.
- Pair each message with the moment it fits (a news story, a community
  meeting, a school parents' group) — relevance beats volume.
- One follow-up maximum. If someone does not engage, stop.
- Never send message 11 or 12 to a group chat, ever. They are private
  one-to-one messages only.
