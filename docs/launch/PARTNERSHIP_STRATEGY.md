# Partnership Strategy

Who to approach, in what order, with what promise — and the promise is
always the same one: **a free, private, verified resource you can give to
the people you serve.** Fast never pays for placement, never trades
endorsements, and never lists an organisation as a partner until it
confirms in writing.

---

## Guiding principles

1. **Relevance over reach.** A single clinic waiting room beats a generic
   national blast. Partnerships start where people already pause.
2. **The site is neutral; the trust is local.** Fast's QR poster next to an
   organisation's logo works because the organisation vouches for it — Fast
   itself stays unbranded and independent.
3. **Nothing to sign, nothing to pay.** Lower the friction to "use it".
   MOUs come later, only if an organisation wants a formal relationship.
4. **Write down every approach.** One simple sheet: organisation, person,
   date sent, response, next step. Never fabricate or imply a response.

## Sequencing (from 25 November)

### Week 1 — The trusted twenty
Ten organisations and ten schools from the founder's and ambassadors' own
networks. Personal emails from [NGO_OUTREACH.md](NGO_OUTREACH.md) and
[SCHOOL_OUTREACH.md](SCHOOL_OUTREACH.md). Goal: five printed posters in
real places and honest feedback.

### Week 2 — The media echo
Local community newspapers and radio ([MEDIA_PITCH.md](MEDIA_PITCH.md)).
Community radio is the strongest channel in the country for exactly this
content — a 5-minute morning-slot interview plus a station WhatsApp line.

### Weeks 3–4 — Institutions
Universities (residences and student-support offices), clinics (waiting
rooms), faith communities (notice sheets), workplaces (HR noticeboards —
16 Days is a natural corporate hook and costs the employer nothing).

### Ongoing — Ambassadors
Each [FAST Community Ambassador](AMBASSADOR_PROGRAM.md) adds one community
at a time, at zero cost, with the code of conduct as the quality gate.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- No organisation's name appears on the site, in the repo, or in any
  material as a partner, supporter or endorser without written confirmation.
- No logos on the website — the directory of real services is the only
  "partnership" Fast displays, and those are service links, not endorsements.
- No exclusivity deals: the same free poster goes to everyone.
- No payment for distribution: if a venue asks for placement fees, the
  answer is a polite no.
- Feedback loop: every partner complaint about content becomes a tracked
  issue in the repository, not a private favour.
