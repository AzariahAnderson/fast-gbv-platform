# QR Distribution Campaign

A QR code is the shortest path from a physical wall to a working crisis
page. It works where posters cannot speak: bathroom stalls, clinic waiting
rooms, hostel noticeboards.

**Two hard rules**

1. **Never generate a QR code from a guessed URL.** Codes are produced only
   after `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` is live and verified, and every code is
   scanned and tested (camera + low light + worn print) before deployment.
   **HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED:** generate all codes from the real production URL.
2. **Every poster carries the plain URL too.** Camera-less phones exist;
   a printed URL is the fallback, never the QR alone.

| NGO offices & shelters | #4 Help someone you care about | Reception and counselling rooms |

**Physical specification**
- Minimum QR print size: 4 × 4 cm for hand cards, 8 × 8 cm for A4 posters.
- Dark ink QR on the light surface only (gold/taupe backgrounds), quiet zone
  preserved, never inverted colours — camera scanners need contrast.
- Include the line "Free · Works offline · No sign-up" beside every code.
- Laminated A4 for indoors; weatherproof board for taxi ranks and outdoor boards.

## The five poster concepts

All posters share: Fast wordmark, Inter type, the four-colour palette
(`#454040` ink, `#605B51` taupe, `#D8D365` gold, `#E6F082` lime), one 3D
animated-style emoji rendered as artwork, the QR code and the line
"Free · Works offline · No sign-up".

### Poster 1 — General Fast
- **Headline:** FAST
- **Subline:** When it hurts most, help answers fast.
- **Body:** Free, confidential GBV support for South Africa. Counselling, shelters, police response, legal protection — all in one calm place, on any phone.
- **Tagline:** Stay close. Stay safe.

### Poster 2 — Need help?
- **Headline:** Need help? Scan. Private. Free.
- **Subline:** Every verified GBV support service in South Africa, on one page — 24 hours a day, even offline.
- **Body line:** No sign-up. Nothing stored. Nothing tracked.
- **Emotion:** direct but calm; this is the bathroom/clinic/stall poster.

### Poster 3 — KNOW THE SIGNS. KNOW WHERE TO GO.
- **Headline:** KNOW THE SIGNS. KNOW WHERE TO GO.
- **Subline:** Warning signs, consent, boundaries, healthy relationships — plain language for young people.
- **Body line:** Free. Anonymous. Works on any phone.
- **Emotion:** educational; the schools and campus poster.

### Poster 4 — Help someone you care about
- **Headline:** Help someone you care about
- **Subline:** What to say, what never to do, and where every free service is. For friends, parents, colleagues and neighbours.
- **Body line:** "I believe you. It's not your fault. I'm here."
- **Emotion:** warm, ally-focused.

### Poster 5 — Save this before you need it
- **Headline:** Save this before you need it
- **Subline:** The GBV Command Centre answers 24/7, free, in all 11 official languages — plus silent channels for when speaking aloud is not safe.
- **Body line:** Save the number on a good day.
- **Emotion:** practical, empowering.

## Production checklist

- ☐ Production URL live and verified (HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED)
- ☐ Codes generated from the final URL (one code, one URL — not per placement)
- ☐ Every code test-scanned from 30 cm, 1.5 m and in dim light
- ☐ Plain URL printed under every code
- ☐ Poster copy approved against the "never says" list in [README.md](README.md)
- ☐ Permission obtained for every private placement (schools, workplaces, clinics)
- ☐ 25 posters + 100 hand-sized cards for the first print run
