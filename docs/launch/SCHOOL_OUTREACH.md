# School Outreach Package

For principals, deputy principals, Life Orientation teachers, school
counsellors, learner-support educators (LSEs) and school governing bodies
(SGBs). Fast gives learners a private, always-available resource — and
gives schools a responsible way to point to it.

**Standing honesty note for every document:** Fast is an independent
public-interest project, not a government service. It carries no advertising,
no sign-up, and stores nothing about learners on any server.

## B. Formal proposal

### 1. What Fast is

Fast (Family Assistance & Safety Technology) is a free, public-interest web
platform for South Africa. One page gathers: the verified emergency
directory (GBV Command Centre, SAPS, emergency services, silent
please-call-me and SMS channels), prevention education, private
safety-planning guidance, sourced statistics and news, and a behaviour-change
programme for people who want to stop harming.

It runs as a Progressive Web App: it loads on weak mobile networks, keeps
working offline once visited, and installs to a phone's home screen like an
app — without an app store, account, or download size barrier.

### 2. Who it is for

Learners (and their families and teachers) who need prevention information,
a private way to understand difficult situations, or immediate access to
verified help — plus those ready to change harmful behaviour.

### 3. What learners can use it for

- Reach the GBV Command Centre and emergency services in one tap.
- Silent channels for when speaking aloud is unsafe.
- Learn the signs of unhealthy and controlling relationships.
- Understand consent and boundaries in plain language.
- Build a private safety plan, step by step.
- Find shelters, counselling, medical and legal support pathways.

### 4. Prevention education

Fast's behaviour-change section addresses learners who bully, control or
harm — and those who want to be better allies. Its live section teaches
media literacy the right way: every statistic carries its publishing
organisation and a real source link, so learners see how claims are checked.

### 5. Safety planning

A calm, private, step-by-step guide — documents, code words, charged phones,
routes planned on good days. Nothing is submitted anywhere; the plan never
leaves the learner's device.

### 6. Support resources

Every support pathway shown on Fast is presented with its service name,
hours and contact channel exactly as the service publishes them. The
emergency directory has a single verified source file in the codebase and an
integrity test that blocks any number appearing anywhere else.

### 7. Emergency access

The crisis call button is persistent on every screen, including the offline
fallback page. A learner who opens Fast with no airtime, no signal and no
history still reaches verified numbers.

### 8. Privacy-first philosophy

No accounts. No cookies for tracking. No personal data collected — the
optional greeting (name and language) lives only in the learner's own
browser storage and is removed with two taps. Fast never knows who opened
it, which is precisely what makes it safe to open.

### 9. PWA accessibility

Works on any phone with a browser — Android or iPhone, new or old. No
storage-permission prompts, no store review, no data-hungry download.

### 10. How a school can distribute Fast

1. Put the QR poster ([SCHOOL_OUTREACH.md poster copy](#d-qr-poster-copy)) on
   noticeboards and inside bathroom stall doors — the places a struggling
   learner can look without being seen.
2. Add one line to the parent WhatsApp group using the ready message in
   [WHATSAPP_CAMPAIGN.md](WHATSAPP_CAMPAIGN.md) (message 8 or 9).
3. A 3-minute assembly introduction (script below) during the 16 Days of
   Activism or any Life Orientation slot.
4. Give counsellors and LSEs the one-pager in
   [NGO_OUTREACH.md](NGO_OUTREACH.md) so the resource is in trusted hands too.
5. Optionally list the address in the school's learner-support directory —
   `[FAST URL]` until the production domain is configured
   (**HUMAN ACTION REQUIRED**).

**What we ask of schools:** nothing except honest feedback. No fees, no
branding requirements, no data sharing. Fast never asks schools for learner
information of any kind.

## C. Assembly introduction (3–4 minutes, teacher/principal script)

> "Good morning, everyone. I want to show you something I wish every school
> in the country had ten years ago.
>
> It's called Fast. It's a website — not an app you download, not something
> you sign up for. You open it on your phone, and it does one thing really
> well: it shows you every free place in South Africa that helps when
> someone is being hurt at home, by a partner, or by anyone — and it
> explains the things we all need to understand: warning signs, consent,
> boundaries, what a healthy relationship looks like.
>
> Here's what makes it different. It works even when you have no airtime
> and no signal. Nobody can see what you looked at — it doesn't ask your
> name, it doesn't store anything, there's nothing to log in to. And the
> emergency numbers on it are the real, verified ones — the Command Centre
> that answers 24 hours a day, free, in all eleven languages.
>
> Why am I showing you this? Because the hardest part of a bad situation is
> often not the situation — it's not knowing where to go. Fast removes
> that. You don't have to be brave on the spot. You can save it quietly
> today and it will be there on the day you, or a friend, need it.
>
> The QR code is on the noticeboard and inside the bathroom doors. Two
> seconds. Save it before you need it. And if you ever help a friend open
> it — you're doing the strongest thing a person can do: staying close.
>
> Stay close. Stay safe."
